Opening Mitosight the first time

Mitosight is free and isn’t sold through Apple, so macOS asks you to approve it once. After that it opens like any other app.

1
Drag Mitosight into ApplicationsIn this window, drag the Mitosight icon onto the Applications folder. Then eject the disk (the Mitosight icon on your desktop or in the Finder sidebar).
2
Double-click Mitosight in ApplicationsmacOS says it could not verify Mitosight. Click Done. (Don’t click Move to Trash.)
3
Open System Settings › Privacy & SecurityApple menu › System Settings… › Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security section, where it says Mitosight was blocked. Click Open Anyway.
4
ConfirmEnter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click Open Anyway or Open in the last message. Mitosight opens.
5
Allow access to your imagesThe first time you open a folder in Documents, Desktop or an external drive, macOS asks whether Mitosight may access it. Click Allow.
On macOS 12, 13 or 14 there’s a shortcut for steps 2 to 4: hold Control, click Mitosight in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

If macOS says Mitosight “is damaged”, open the Terminal app, paste this line and press Return, then open Mitosight again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Mitosight.app

Updates install themselves: the version and an Update now button appear at the top right of the window. Your experiments and results are kept; they live next to your images.

Notifications: the first time an analysis runs, macOS asks whether Mitosight may send notifications. Click Allow to be told when it finishes.

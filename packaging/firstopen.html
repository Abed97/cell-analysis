Opening CycleTrack the first time

CycleTrack is free and isn’t sold through Apple, so macOS asks you to approve it once. After that it opens like any other app.

1
Drag CycleTrack into ApplicationsIn this window, drag the CycleTrack icon onto the Applications folder. Then eject the disk (the CycleTrack icon on your desktop or in the Finder sidebar).
2
Double-click CycleTrack in ApplicationsmacOS says it could not verify CycleTrack. Click Done. (Don’t click Move to Trash.)
3
Open System Settings › Privacy & SecurityApple menu › System Settings… › Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the Security section, where it says CycleTrack was blocked. Click Open Anyway.
4
ConfirmEnter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click Open Anyway or Open in the last message. CycleTrack opens.
5
Allow access to your imagesThe first time you open a folder in Documents, Desktop or an external drive, macOS asks whether CycleTrack may access it. Click Allow.
On macOS 12, 13 or 14 there’s a shortcut for steps 2 to 4: hold Control, click CycleTrack in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

If macOS says CycleTrack “is damaged”, open the Terminal app, paste this line and press Return, then open CycleTrack again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CycleTrack.app

Updates install themselves: the version and an Update now button appear at the top right of the window. Your experiments and results are kept; they live next to your images.

Notifications: the first time an analysis runs, macOS asks whether CycleTrack may send notifications. Click Allow to be told when it finishes.
